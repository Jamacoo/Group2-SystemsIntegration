Download the extension

How to install it

  1. Find the downloaded zip file, right-click it, and choose Extract All. Put the folder somewhere you won't delete, such as Documents.
  2. Open Chrome or Edge. In the address bar, type chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (the switch at the top right, or the left menu in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder (the one that contains manifest.json).
  5. Go back to your exam link and reload the page.

Works on Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera. Do not move or delete the folder afterward. If your browser shows a "developer mode extensions" warning when it starts, keep the extension enabled.
