Setup Guide

Running an exam

  1. In the Admin Panel under Add a New Exam, enter a name and your Google Form's embed link. To get it, open your Google Form, click Send, choose the <> (embed) tab, and copy only the address inside src="...".
  2. In Share with Students, copy the extension link and send it to your students first. The page it opens explains how to install the extension.
  3. Then send each student the exam's own link from the exam list. They must install the extension before they start.
  4. During the exam, watch the Dashboard and Live Activity pages.
