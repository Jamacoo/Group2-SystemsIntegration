Setup Guide

Running an exam

  1. In the Admin Panel under Add a New Exam, enter a name and your Google Form's link. To get it, open your Google Form, click Publish, and click Copy responder link.
  2. In Share with Students, copy the extension link and send it to your students first. The page it opens explains how to install the extension.
  3. Then send each student the exam's own link from your exam list. They must install the extension before they start.
  4. During the exam, watch the Dashboard and Live Activity pages. Use the Exam filter on the dashboard to look at one exam at a time.
